Useful Travel Information

Know the essentials before you go.

General travel notes for El Salvador, designed to help visitors prepare safely and accessibly without adding another page to the menu.

Emergency Numbers

Police emergencies: 911. Ambulance: 132. Save these before leaving your hotel or meeting point.

Money and Payments

The U.S. dollar is the official currency. Carry small bills because some places may not accept cards.

Transportation

Use planned transport for longer routes, avoid intercity driving at night and check road conditions during rain.

Weather

Dry season is usually November to April. Rainy season is usually May to October, with heavier travel risks on mountain roads.

Entry and Documents

Travelers should carry a valid passport and confirm visa or entry rules based on their nationality. Visitors entering the CA-4 region may have a shared 90-day stay limit across El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras and Nicaragua.

Tourist Assistance

El Salvador Travel lists Tourist Assistance Centers in destinations such as San Salvador, Suchitoto, La Libertad, Ruta de Las Flores and the international airport. Many operate Monday to Sunday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Health and Safety

Pack medication, sunscreen, water and any personal accessibility items. For outdoor routes, check weather warnings and avoid unfamiliar roads at night, especially in rural or mountainous areas.

Accessibility Planning

Before booking, confirm ramps, accessible restrooms, parking distance, guide support and whether a destination has sign language or audio information. Accessibility can change by venue or season.

Need help confirming a route?

Ask before you travel if you need wheelchair access, interpreter support, low-walking routes or confirmed restrooms.
