Our Commitment

Accessible tourism information for everyone

Open Routes aims to help people explore El Salvador with clearer information about destinations, routes, accessibility support and travel planning. We design the website with accessibility in mind so visitors can navigate, read and plan with fewer barriers.

Our goal is to keep improving the experience for users who rely on keyboard navigation, visual adjustments, audio support, sign language content or clearer route information before traveling.

Available Tools

Accessibility features currently included

High contrast and dark mode

Users can adjust the visual appearance of the site from the floating accessibility button.

Adjustable text size

The text size control allows users to increase or reduce reading size.

Audio guide support

The voice assistant can read page content and help users navigate with spoken commands when enabled.

Sign language resources

Open Routes includes a sign language navigation menu and destination videos where available.

Destination access details

Destination pages include accessibility checklists, practical information and route notes.

Known Limitations

What we are still improving

  • Not every destination currently has a sign language video.
  • Some maps and external content may not be fully controlled by Open Routes.
  • Real-world accessibility conditions can change, so travelers should confirm details before visiting.
  • Some booking and payment features are demo flows and do not process real payments yet.
  • Some destination accessibility information depends on restaurants, guides, venues or local providers.
Feedback and Support

Report an accessibility problem

If you find something difficult to use, unclear or inaccessible, please contact Open Routes. Helpful details include the page name, the problem you found, the device or browser you used and the support you need.
